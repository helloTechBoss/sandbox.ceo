## Phase 1 — Technical Setup (Week 1)

### 1.1 Google Search Console
**Priority:** Critical — must do before anything else

**Steps:**
1. Go to [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Click **Add Property** → choose **Domain** type → enter `sandbox.ceo`
3. Copy the DNS TXT verification record Google gives you
4. Log in to your domain registrar (where you bought sandbox.ceo) → DNS settings → add TXT record
5. Click **Verify** in Search Console (may take up to 24 hours)
6. Once verified, go to **Sitemaps** → submit: `https://www.sandbox.ceo/sitemap.xml`
7. Check **Coverage** tab after 3–5 days to confirm pages are being indexed

**What to monitor weekly:**
- **Performance** tab → which queries bring impressions/clicks
- **Coverage** → any pages with errors or warnings
- **Core Web Vitals** → page speed signals

## Phase 4 — Monitoring & Optimisation (Month 3–6)

### 4.1 Monthly SEO Review

**Every month, check:**

1. **Google Search Console → Performance**
   - Which queries have impressions but low CTR? → improve title/meta for those pages
   - Which pages are getting clicks? → add more content to them
   - Any new query opportunities? → consider new landing pages

2. **Google Business Profile insights**
   - How many profile views, calls, website clicks?
   - Which search queries show your profile?

3. **Ranking check (manual)**
   - Search these in incognito mode from Hong Kong:
     - "MSO licence application Hong Kong"
     - "MSO licence renewal Hong Kong"
     - "outsourced compliance officer Hong Kong"
     - "AML audit Hong Kong"
     - "company incorporation Hong Kong fintech"
   - Note position. Aim to be on page 1 within 6 months for niche queries.

4. **Review count** — are you getting 1–2 new reviews per month?
